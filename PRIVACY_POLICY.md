# Privacy Policy – MeiOCRWorkout

MeiOCRWorkout is an Android application for creating and recording personal OCR workout sessions.

## Data collection and use
MeiOCRWorkout does not use advertising or analytics and does not sell personal data.

Workout profiles, workout configuration, workout history, and optional group-training participant names/colors are stored locally on the user's device. Users may optionally use community features backed by Firebase.

The application is intended to use Firebase Crashlytics to receive technical crash reports and diagnose app errors. Crash reports may contain technical information such as device and operating-system information, app version and state, stack traces, and diagnostic identifiers. Crashlytics is not used for advertising or analytics.

Google may process technical information required to provide Firebase Crashlytics. For information about Google's handling of data, see Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy and security documentation.

## Local data
Workout profiles, workout history, and group-training participant metadata remain on the device by default. If a user explicitly submits a profile for community publication, that profile plus publication metadata (such as description, location text, tags, Firebase user ID, display name, and account email for moderation purposes) is transmitted to Firebase. Imported community profiles are copied into local storage.

## Third parties
MeiOCRWorkout does not contain advertising SDKs and does not use Firebase Analytics or other analytics/tracking services.

The application uses Google Play services for app-update functionality, Firebase Authentication for optional sign-in, Cloud Firestore for community profile discovery/submissions, Firebase Cloud Functions for moderation/admin actions, and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/

## Data sharing
Personal workout history is not sold or shared with third parties. Profiles are shared publicly only when the user explicitly submits them and they are approved by moderation. Account/profile-submission metadata and technical crash information may be processed by Google/Firebase to provide the requested services.

## Data deletion
Locally stored workout data can be deleted using the application's available deletion functions, by clearing the app data, or by uninstalling the application. Community submissions/publications are separate cloud records; deletion/withdrawal controls can be added as the community feature evolves.

## Security
Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## Children
MeiOCRWorkout is not specifically directed at children.

## Changes
This privacy policy may be updated when the application's functionality or services change. The current version is published in this repository.
